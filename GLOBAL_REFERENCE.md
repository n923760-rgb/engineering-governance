## Fixed target-project storage

- `/ENGINEERING/MASTER_ROADMAP.md` — one canonical roadmap.
- `/ENGINEERING/REPORTS/` — detailed reports.
- `/ENGINEERING/EVIDENCE/` — evidence indexes/metadata.

Do not create a competing roadmap elsewhere in the same project.

## First-round rule

Every newly adopted or re-baselined project begins with a **read-only Master Re-baseline**. No source mutation or roadmap write belongs in that first round.

The first formal deliverable is the `MASTER ENGINEERING BASELINE REPORT`, containing a proposed initial/updated roadmap state.

## Five mandatory questions

1. What exact source are we working from?
2. What execution capabilities are actually available?
3. Who authorized this exact action?
4. What evidence proves the result?
5. What actions were intentionally not performed or were blocked?

## Target-project rule

This repository is the **reference**, not the source of live truth for another project. Derive target facts live.
