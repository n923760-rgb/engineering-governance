## First-round rule

Every newly adopted or re-baselined project begins with a **read-only Master Re-baseline**. No source mutation belongs in that first round.

The first formal deliverable is the `MASTER ENGINEERING BASELINE REPORT`.

## Five mandatory questions

1. What exact source are we working from?
2. Who authorized this exact action?
3. What changed and why?
4. What evidence proves the result?
5. What actions were intentionally not performed?

## Target-project rule

This repository is the **reference**, not the source of live truth for another project. Derive target facts live.
